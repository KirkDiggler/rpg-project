Composable Pieces

The engine knows a few pieces. An arrest, a jailing, a guarded door and a shared conversation are recipes an author joins from them. Today the engine knows the recipes too, each with its own rules.

This page is for thinking, dated 2026-11-17. The first two figures are what three scouts read in the engine as it stands. The rest is a proposed shape to pick apart. Nothing here is built and nothing is decided.

1. Today: one arrest, written five times

Follow one thing: the party is arrested at the gate. The engine records that single event in five places, because each reader can only see one kind of record.

Teaching a fact exists five ways, differing only in who learns it and what sets it off. One thing in the castle is already built from general pieces alone, with no engine word: the gate’s “vouch” line. Composition works wherever the pieces allow it.

3. A proposed shape: four kinds of piece

Read it as a loop. Something wakes a machine. Its step does things to someone. That makes things true. What is true decides the next step, and everything else in the world can read it.

What exists in some form

  • Beats. A creature’s machine already hears a dozen. A conversation hears only one.
  • A machine. One engine runs behaviours and conversations. A second runs conditions.
  • Most consequences. Say, teach, recognise, move and hand over all have shared writers underneath.
  • Known by someone, and about someone. Facts and topics, in two separate stores.

What would be new

  • One list. The same consequences on a row, a step, a door or a watch.
  • Who. The speaker, those in sight, those in an area, a remembered group.
  • True of the world, and readable by all. Including by movement and by anything that waits.
  • Hold in place. As a general consequence, ended by a beat such as a fight starting.
  • Parameters. A machine written once and used with different places and words.

4. The gate’s arrest, drawn as a recipe

The same castle story, written with the pieces above. The machine is about one party. The engine would know nothing called arrest or jail.

What this cut would retire as engine words: arrest, jail, sceneMove, the custody phases, the reserved topics arrested, wanted and jailed, and the facts that exist only to bridge them. What it must not lose: the split is decided once and kept; a fight is a way out; someone who walked off before the arrest is still wanted.

5. A conversation, pulled apart

A conversation holds no lines. The lines stay on each person. What it holds today is three things that may not belong together.

7. Open, for the operator

Three questions shape everything else. They are asked one at a time on the story issue.

  1. Whose is a conversation’s standing? The whole run, as today, or the party or person it is about.
  2. Is “true about someone” one thing? Facts and topics become one kind of truth that every reader sees, or stay two stores with a bridge.
  3. One machine? Behaviours, conversations and conditions run on one mechanism that can sense, act and take parameters, or stay separate with a shared list of consequences between them.
