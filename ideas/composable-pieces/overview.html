Composable Pieces

Authors build their stories from a few reusable pieces. No piece is named after a story. An arrest, a jailing and a feast are things an author writes, not words the engine knows.

The first two figures are what three scouts read in the engine as it stands. The rest is the shape the operator ruled on, one question at a time, on 2026-11-18, and two stories written in it to test it. Nothing here is built. The engine’s current shape is free to change.

1. Today: one arrest, written five times

Follow one thing: the party is arrested at the gate. The engine records that single event in five places, because each reader can only see one kind of record.

3. The shape: six pieces

A machine tells a story. Its states do things to people. That makes facts so, and gives people things to hold. The author writes how what is so gets out. People act only on what they hold.

PieceWhat it isRuled
FactSomething that is so in the world, with no point of view: the feast is tonight, the village burned.Facts are a plain list, kept apart from what people hold.
HoldingSomeone holds something, optionally about someone. The guards hold “arrested” about us. We hold “is-chained” on ourselves, and whoever sees us comes to hold it too.Arrested is a holding. A status is a holding on the person it shows on. Jailed is not stored: it is where you are.
SenseThe channel a fact or a holding comes in on: heard or seen. Telling is not a sense. It is someone saying it, and whoever hears it comes to hold it.The author determines how a fact gets out. Nothing becomes known by itself. A sense is the channel.
ConsequenceWhat a machine’s step puts out. Done once on entering a state, or in force while the state lasts. Authors pick them; the engine team composes them from smaller primitives.Hold-in-place is the only real condition, and it must end with the hold. A condition comes from the effects system, with how it ends stated explicitly so it cannot get stuck.
WhoWhich people a consequence applies to: the ones in this area, the ones who saw it, the ones someone holds a thing about.The author picks the holders. No group is stored: the holdings are the memory.
MachineStates and the beats between them. It may belong to a creature, to a conversation, or to nobody. This is where authors find the magic.The machine tells the story and processes everyone. Each person’s standing is the holdings about them.

4. First test: the gate’s arrest, written in the pieces

Everything the castle’s front gate does today, with no engine word called arrest or jail. Fail two claims and the gate arrests whoever is there; go quietly and you are walked to the cells; fight and the hold is gone; walk off and you are wanted.

What fires the jailing. Not a machine. The line “I’ll come quietly” puts out one consequence, a move, for the speaker. The move is a status effect the engine team composed from primitive effects (section 3). The hold is a separate status effect, and how it ends is stated on it.

What writing it out showed:

  • No stored group. Someone seized later joins by being given the same holding. The separate arrest record goes away.
  • Two forms of consequence. Once on entering, or in force while the state lasts. The hold is the second form, so it cannot outlive what holds it.
  • The author no longer needs a jailed state to make the move happen. Going quietly is a line that moves the speaker. An author may still write such a state if the story wants one.

What this would retire as engine words: arrest, jail, sceneMove, the custody phases, the reserved topics arrested, wanted and jailed, and the facts that exist only to bridge them.

5. Second test: the feast and the burned village

A story with nothing in common with the gate, built on the operator’s two facts. It needed no new consequence. It did need a machine that belongs to nobody, and it shows why “is so” and “someone holds it” are kept apart.

6. A conversation, pulled apart

A conversation holds no lines. The lines stay on each person. What it holds today is three things that may not belong together.

7. Tools the studio could offer

If the pieces are few and general, the studio’s job changes from one form per engine word to a small set of tools that work on any recipe.

ToolWhat an author does with itWhat it needs from the engine
Machine canvasSee a machine as states and steps. Click a step to read its beat, its condition and what it does. Add a state, draw a step.The engine says each step’s from, to and beat as connections (rpg-project #985).
Fill in a recipePick an author’s “arrest at a gate” from a shelf and fill in the area, the cell and the words. The form is made from the recipe’s parameters.Machines that take parameters. The engine publishes each recipe’s parameter list.

What can go hereEvery dropdown lists what the engine accepts at that spot, and nothing typed into the studio’s own code.The engine publishes its pieces: consequences, beats, who-words, and where each is allowed.
The shelfKeep recipes and pieces: ours, the author’s own, and an add-on’s, each with a version.A unit of sharing below a whole site (rpg-project #977).

8. Ruled, and still open

Ruled by the operator, 2026-11-18

  • Facts and what people hold are two things. Facts are a plain list with no point of view.
  • Arrested is a holding: what matters is who holds it and whether they pass it on.
  • Only “held” is a condition, and it must end when the hold is over. The player can always fight out.
  • Jailed is where you are. A status such as is-chained is held on the person it shows on.
  • The machine tells the story and processes everyone. People’s standing is the holdings about them.
  • The author determines how a fact gets out.
  • A sense is the channel it comes in on: heard, seen. Telling is saying it.
  • The machine is the author’s. Making consequences is the engine team’s job, composed from primitives and the effects system.

Still open

  • Passing it on. How one holder gives what they hold to another, and whether it can change on the way.
  • One machine? Behaviours, conversations and the ownerless kind on one mechanism, or separate with the consequences shared.
  • The who-words. The short list an author picks from, and whether authors can name their own.
  • Reuse. Writing a machine once and using it at a second gate with a different cell and words.
