Reuse the foundation; add the missing owner

Already present

  • A post holds the opening and stops a long walk.
  • The guard reacts to a bump and speaks in the same act.
  • Speak supports authored checks and answers.
  • Movement, persistence and machine inspection have real consumers.

Proposed additions

  • One shared interaction, driven by the existing machine.
  • A recoverable, addressed invitation that does not freeze unrelated play.
  • Versioned choices and reservation through a paused check.
  • Explicit consequences, distinct from who knows about them.

The distinctions that matter

Not the same thingWhy
Invitation / paused checkOpening replies must not freeze distant players. An actual check may still use its existing resolution window.
Shared attempt / personal knowledgeA different speaker gets no fresh budget; a distant member is not taught dialogue they never heard.
Entry granted / guard arrivedPassage releases without waiting for the guard to finish walking to the ballroom.
Panel dismissed / entrance resolvedClosing the UI spends no check and grants no permission.

What still needs a ruling

  • For the intel branch of arrest: how known-location memories differ from lost or unknown locations. The area-or-intel boundary itself is settled.
  • How the authored approach and physical bypass meet the existing post-at-place rule.
  • Final claim mapping and the boundary of second-attempt advantage removal.
  • The proposed state, selection and lifecycle contracts before implementation.
