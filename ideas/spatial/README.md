# ideas/spatial — geometry, placement, and search

The grouping for designs about **where things are and what that means**: grids,
footprints, coverage, distance, sight, and the search that runs over them. The
owning code is `rpg-toolkit/tools/spatial` (geometry, no rules) and the
derived cell facts in `rpg-toolkit/rulebooks/dnd5e/encounter` (rules, no
geometry of its own).

| design | status | one line |
|---|---|---|
| [foundation](foundation/design.md) | DESIGN, proposed 2026-09-11 | footprint on the definition · coverage and distance field in spatial · cell facts derived in encounter |

Older spatial-flavoured designs still sit at the top of `ideas/` and are
candidates to move here when they are next touched, not before:
`coordinate-types`, `dungeon-walls`, `multi-room-dungeons`, `fog-of-war`,
`perceive`.
