The first field note ended with a Barbarian and a question.

Well, Rage is part of that data, right?

The answer became the next layer of the toolkit.

I needed rules that could survive being saved, return as behavior, and react to the game without combat knowing each one by name.

The path ran through Ref, an external Artificer, Rage, and then Bless.

It became a pattern I still use: build the smallest useful seam, give it the hardest real example, then give it the next one. Keep going until the connections no longer fit in my head.

Sometimes the abstraction survived. Sometimes it deadlocked. Eventually it produced AttackChain.On(bus).

That line let me sleep. When I saw it, I knew we were going to be okay.

This second architecture field note is the story of that nervous system:

https://kirkdiggler.github.io/rpg-toolkit/the-nervous-system/

It ends at a limit we could not see yet.

The event bus could ask every rule in memory. It could not wait for a person who was not there yet.
