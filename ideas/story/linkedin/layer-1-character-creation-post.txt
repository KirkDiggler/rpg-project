I said I was going to peel this project back one layer at a time, so I am starting at the beginning.

The first idea was a polling adventure bot for Discord, inspired by the Mastodon bot I had contributed to. Then I learned Discord bots could have forms. Suddenly character creation was possible.

I thought the open D&D data from dnd5eapi.co could drive the whole flow.

Then I tried to make a Monk.

That is where I learned that not all data is equal. Some data carries values. Some data quietly asks every consumer to become an interpreter. At some point, configuration becomes a programming language.

This first field note is about that lesson, the relief of coding what a Fighter actually gets, and the ToData seam that finally made characters simple to save and load:

https://kirkdiggler.github.io/rpg-toolkit/

It ends with a Barbarian and the next problem hiding inside its data.
