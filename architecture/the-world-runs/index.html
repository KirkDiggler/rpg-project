The World Runs

A creature is a machine that listens. When the party moves, the engine wakes only the creatures that are due or addressed, in seat order, in one pass. The roster is never walked.

This page is the overview for 2026-11-01. The design document is the law; this is the map of it, with where each piece sits in the stack, what it has cost and saved so far, and the day's order of work.

1. The mechanism: a raise wakes who is due

A creature is a machine. Its mind is a current state and, for that state, what it listens to and what it does when that fires. All of it is data: on a beat, when a guard holds, do acts, to a state.

The raise wakes who is due or addressed. Two indexes, rebuilt at load from the machines' states, never persisted. The cost of a Move grows with who woke, never with the roster.

Clocks are content. world is the default. A fight is a clock. A tomb can declare its own. A portal is a transfer. Every key the engine holds names its clock.

Reproducible, not seeded. Dice stay as random as they can be. A replay reads the record's own rolls back in order.

No row gets worse. Every shipped table is a one-state machine whose time transition rolls it. The castle plays the same.
