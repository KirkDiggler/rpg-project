# Run — 2026-11-26 — Rulings wait as issues; the story is the session's page

The operator's direction, on asking where R4 was: an artifact for open questions and rulings needing a look, with the code they decide on; a feature as an issue whose children are its waves and slices, carried by a session, showing what is played. The story: [rpg-project#1237](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/issues/1237).

## Shipped

- The design skill: an open ruling is an issue labelled `ruling` in the repository that owns its consequences; the table points at it; no checklist in a thread — [https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/pull/1239](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/pull/1239).
- The story skill: a story shows what has been played, is carried by a session, and its open rulings are issues — [https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/pull/1239](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/pull/1239).
- The mechanism, used before it was written: the label, [R3](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-dnd5e-unreal/issues/2) and [R9](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-dnd5e-unreal/issues/3) waiting in the client's repository, R4 and R8 entered in the table, the story with its playtest [#1238](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/issues/1238) as a sub-issue — [rpg-project#1237](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/issues/1237).

## Decided

- One artifact for rulings, the design's table, with open rows linking to their issues; not a second file per repository, because two copies of a decision are how "where is R4" comes to be asked — [https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/pull/1239](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/pull/1239).
- An open ruling lives with the code it decides on, in the repository that owns its consequences, as the operator asked; the story lives in rpg-project, where its slices can be its sub-issues — [rpg-dnd5e-unreal#2](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-dnd5e-unreal/issues/2), [rpg-project#1237](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/issues/1237).
- O2 became R9, so that every open question has one kind of number; O3 is settled by R5 to R8 — [rpg-project#1155](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/pull/1155).
- Frames with licensed models are neither committed to a public repository nor embedded from a private one; whether they may be shown at all is the operator's call, written as a line in the story — [rpg-project#1237](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/issues/1237).
