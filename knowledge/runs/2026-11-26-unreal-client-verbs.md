## Decided

- The client names the straight hex line and the engine walks it cell by cell, stopping with its reason; the client finds no route and the contract gains no pathfinding call, because that is the contract as the operator stated it in conversation on 2026-11-26 and as the engine behaves — [evidence-2026-11-26-motion.log](../../ideas/unreal-client/spike/unreal/evidence-2026-11-26-motion.log).
- A figure moves only on what the record says, never on a verb's response, so the player's walk and a monster's are read by one function; it rests on the law *Intent in, record out* — [design.md](../../ideas/unreal-client/design.md#law).
- The automation tests use runs the engine-side test launches, not `StartGame`, because that server registers `SessionService` and `RecordService` only; seat recovery stays with the play lab on the real stack — [round-verbs.md](../../ideas/unreal-client/spike/round-verbs.md).
- A click is the ray under the cursor met with the floor's plane, rounded to a hex, because the floor is a procedural mesh without collision; a click on a cell where a figure stands swings at it — [RpgPlayLabActor.cpp](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/blob/f24359d38617b4c9b30f833ddc621efcd94f6f08/ideas/unreal-client/spike/unreal/RpgAssetLab/RpgPlayLabActor.cpp) — [e6a3b8a](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/commit/e6a3b8a).
- A verb from a player's hand goes at once and a scripted one keeps its pause; a click while the figure is still walking is held back, because the next path must start from the cell the record last named — [RpgPlayLabActor.cpp](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/blob/f24359d38617b4c9b30f833ddc621efcd94f6f08/ideas/unreal-client/spike/unreal/RpgAssetLab/RpgPlayLabActor.cpp) — [e6a3b8a](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/commit/e6a3b8a).
- Outside a fight, `E` reads "outside a fight there is no turn to end": the engine's own answer through `Afford`, said plainly; the client computes nothing to say it — [RpgPlayLabActor.cpp](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/blob/f24359d38617b4c9b30f833ddc621efcd94f6f08/ideas/unreal-client/spike/unreal/RpgAssetLab/RpgPlayLabActor.cpp) — [e6a3b8a](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/commit/e6a3b8a).
- The scene lab starts only once it holds a target, in a game or in the editor, because an actor in a game begins play as it is spawned, before its spawner can read it one — [RpgSceneLabActor.cpp](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/blob/f24359d38617b4c9b30f833ddc621efcd94f6f08/ideas/unreal-client/spike/unreal/RpgAssetLab/RpgSceneLabActor.cpp) — [a0795c4](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/commit/a0795c4).

## Found

- A `Move` path names the cells to step onto; the engine refuses one that begins on the cell the member stands on — [evidence-2026-11-26-motion.log](../../ideas/unreal-client/spike/unreal/evidence-2026-11-26-motion.log).
- The world clock ticks when a swing lands, and every driven member on the board moves in that one tick: 57 to 75 record beats at once, and gate-1 walks seven cells toward the fight it heard — [evidence-2026-11-26-motion.log](../../ideas/unreal-client/spike/unreal/evidence-2026-11-26-motion.log).
- A target out of reach is refused inside `Afford`, the declaration withheld with "no target in reach"; on the world clock `Afford` offers no Move or End Turn rows at all, and offers the bard six spells — [evidence-2026-11-26-verbs.log](../../ideas/unreal-client/spike/unreal/evidence-2026-11-26-verbs.log).
- The beats as written: `moved` carries a member and the cell arrived at; `struck` and `missed` carry the actor, its targets, the roll, the total and the armour class; `turn-ended` carries who ended and who is next; `stayed` carries why a driven member did not move — [evidence-2026-11-26-verbs.log](../../ideas/unreal-client/spike/unreal/evidence-2026-11-26-verbs.log).
- The burst costs a maximum frame of 10.3 ms and a p99 of 9.0 ms against 11.3 ms idle, uncapped and offscreen; a screenshot request costs its frame 70 to 130 ms; one hitch of about 350 ms sits half a second after the scene builds — [evidence-2026-11-26-frames.log](../../ideas/unreal-client/spike/unreal/evidence-2026-11-26-frames.log).
- An Unreal module on Linux links with undefined symbols: a function declared and never defined builds, and is a module that fails to load, caught only by running it — [round-verbs.md](../../ideas/unreal-client/spike/round-verbs.md).
- `APlayerController::InputKeyBindings` is gone in 5.9; a key binds on the controller's input component, and `FInputKeyEventArgs` takes a device id and a timestamp — [RpgPlayLabActor.cpp](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/blob/f24359d38617b4c9b30f833ddc621efcd94f6f08/ideas/unreal-client/spike/unreal/RpgAssetLab/RpgPlayLabActor.cpp).
- On a pointy-topped hex board the cell `(q, r+1)` is the south-east neighbour, and due south is a vertex 100 cm out, not an edge — [RpgMotionTest.cpp](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/blob/f24359d38617b4c9b30f833ddc621efcd94f6f08/ideas/unreal-client/spike/unreal/RpgAssetLab/Tests/RpgMotionTest.cpp).
- The operator played it by hand the day it was built: clicked and moved, and saw the guards take their patrol turn — [rpg-project#1155](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/pull/1155).

## Mistaken

- The play lab's rewrite left out the skeletal-mesh header and compiled only by amalgamation; the per-file check caught it once the compile database covered the round's new file, and one commit had claimed that check before it ran — [bb37f6d](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/commit/bb37f6d).
- The first turn marker did not render, for two reasons found one at a time: it was built without the recipe the scene lab's walls use, and a marker spawned in the frame of a screenshot is a package still loading — [a0795c4](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/commit/a0795c4), [3a6ec91](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/commit/3a6ec91).
- A test was written expecting the cell due south of another to be its neighbour; the author's picture of the board was wrong and the test was corrected before the code it tests existed — [e6a3b8a](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/commit/e6a3b8a).
- Building while an editor was open left a stale hot-reload library that the next run loaded in place of the module; the tests then ran one of fourteen — [round-verbs.md](../../ideas/unreal-client/spike/round-verbs.md).
