## Decided

- S3 in two: the shapes now, the content after the store gains an Unreal plugin — S4 in rpg-game-assets, which has none today, S5 in the client, resolving `PublicMemberInfo.appearance_ref` — the operator's go on 2026-11-28 — [rpg-project#1266, section 1](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/issues/1266).
- The standard is the web's, exactly, and friend from foe by the engine's word alone: the contract's closed stance vocabulary is `hostile`, `neutral`, `allied`; `neutral` is no colour word, and the roster's faction colours nothing because a faction is a name — [the disposition](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/issues/1266#issuecomment-7231821339).
- The word's lifecycle: `View` after ready, every act and every sight change, the last view cached per member, a figure placed later taking its cached word at once — [the disposition](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/issues/1266#issuecomment-7232267349).
- A lit shape is judged by its footprint, never its centre pixel; a picture oracle names where; a frame is one moment and its oracle is that moment's log — [the dispositions](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/issues/1266#issuecomment-7232505880).
- The stand-in is unlit by design, because the ruling says flat colour and a lit surface is albedo times whatever the light delivers; the five guessed film-curve parameters were reverted and the pass switched off — [the disposition](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/issues/1266#issuecomment-7233069791).
- The fixture is served from the commit it is pinned to, `40da1fc3`, never from a moving branch — [the disposition](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/issues/1266#issuecomment-7232188724).

## Found

- S2's floor material had never loaded: `/Engine/EngineMaterials/VertexColorMaterial` does not exist in this engine, every run had logged `Warning: Failed to find object`, and no gate read the run log — [the disposition](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/issues/1266#issuecomment-7231978711).
- `/Engine/EngineDebugMaterials/VertexColorMaterial` is Default Lit, `ShadingModelField = 2`; the lead said unlit from one observation and the builder read the asset — [the gap](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/issues/1266#issuecomment-7232803782).
- A material's `VertexColor` node returns the stored bytes as linear: sRGB bytes stored read as linear gamma-misread every colour, and the floor matched only because the gain was fitted to it — [the disposition](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/issues/1266#issuecomment-7232902845).
- A filmic tone curve lifts dark channels and compresses bright ones — per-channel linear ratios of 1.30, 1.13 and 0.81 on a figure the gain could not fix — [the disposition](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/issues/1266#issuecomment-7233000435).
- The installed `grpcurl` is a snap whose home interface is not connected: it reads neither `/tmp` nor `$HOME`; the engine's own response, logged before any mapping, is the better oracle — [the disposition](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/issues/1266#issuecomment-7232120532).
- The castle fixture's pedlar is `MEMBER_KIND_MONSTER` with `stance` `neutral`, standing up, and the tomb's driven turn plays in the same instant as the end of the fighter's — [the capture](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/issues/1266#issuecomment-7232480476).
